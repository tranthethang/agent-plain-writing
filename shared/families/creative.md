## Voice family

Numeric limits are per language and type. They live only in `languages/<lang>/profile.json`. The type section may relax or tighten a rule. See "Who owns what" for which part wins.

### `creative` (`essay`)

- **Sentence length**: `sentence` in `profile.json`.
- **Paragraph length**: `paragraph` in `profile.json`, one idea.
- **Rhythm and imagery**: Owned by the type section.
- **Evaluative adjectives**: None without a visible detail.
- **Inner voice**: the facts stay fixed (core rule 5), but the narrator may think, remember and doubt. Use the inner material the brief gives first. When the brief gives none, the type section sets how much you may add. Report each added thought outside the text (Workflow step 7).

**Paragraph form:** Write sentences continuously inside a paragraph, and never split every sentence onto its own line (no trailing-space `  \n` line breaks). Sentence rhythm is set by the type file.
