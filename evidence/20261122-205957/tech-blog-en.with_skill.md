# A 20 kg alert gave our bakery time to restock

Last month, I replaced paper order slips with a shared online sheet and low-stock alerts. Before the switch, we ran out of flour twice in three weeks.

## Paper slips hid low stock

In those same three weeks, we threw away 18 kg of unsold dough. I also made two emergency trips to a wholesale store, each taking 40 minutes.

Paper slips hid what was running low until the shelf was empty. With one shared sheet, all three staff members could update the stock.

## The first alert came too late

I first set the flour alert at 10 kg. We ran out once more in the first week.

Our supplier takes two days to deliver, and we use about 8 kg of flour each day. I moved the alert to 20 kg with that delivery time in mind.

## A quick check at closing

In the four weeks after the change, we did not run out of stock. Waste was 4 kg per week.

Staff missed two sheet updates in the first week, so I taped a printed reminder next to the till. Checking the sheet takes five minutes at closing time.

Set your alert with your supplier’s delivery time and your daily use in mind, then check the sheet at closing.
