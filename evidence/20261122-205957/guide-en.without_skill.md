# Export a Daily Sales Report in KoraPOS

You can export a daily sales summary as a CSV file from KoraPOS on an iPad or Android tablet.

## Export the report

1. Open **KoraPOS** and tap **Reports** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap **Daily Sales**.
3. Choose one date from the calendar. You can select a date up to 90 days back.
4. Tap **Export CSV**.
5. Choose **Save to Files** or **Send to Email**.
6. Wait up to 15 seconds for the file to appear. Its name will be `sales-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`, with the selected date in the filename.

## If the export doesn’t work

- **Nothing happens after you tap Export CSV:** Connect the tablet to Wi-Fi, then tap **Export CSV** again.
- **The file is empty (0 bytes):** There are no paid orders for that date. Pick a different date.
- **Send to Email fails:** Ask the manager to add the store email under **Settings** > **Store Profile**.
