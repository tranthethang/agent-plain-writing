# Export a daily sales summary

Export a daily sales summary as a CSV file from KoraPOS.

## Steps

1. Open **KoraPOS** on your iPad or Android tablet.
2. Tap **Reports** in the bottom bar.
3. Select **Daily Sales**.
4. Pick one date from the calendar. You can go back up to 90 days.
5. Tap **Export CSV**.
6. Choose **Save to Files** or **Send to Email**.
7. Wait up to 15 seconds for `sales-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` to appear.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens after **Export CSV** | Connect the tablet to Wi-Fi, then tap **Export CSV** again. |
| The file is empty (0 bytes) | No paid orders exist for that date. Pick a different date. |
| **Send to Email** fails | The store email is not set. Ask your manager to add it under **Settings** > **Store Profile**. |
