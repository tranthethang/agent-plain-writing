# The Desk Fan

In my first week at university, my rented room in the new city was bare. My suitcase sat in the corner, its broken zipper tied shut with a piece of twine. For three days, it was the only thing in the room.

My father had repaired an old desk fan and packed it in a cardboard box. He said nothing about money. He only told me to change the fuse if the motor ran hot. I still wonder whether he meant to say more.

On the third afternoon, I plugged in the fan. The blades turned with a soft hum. Air moved through the room and lifted the loose end of twine on my suitcase. It settled back against the zipper.
