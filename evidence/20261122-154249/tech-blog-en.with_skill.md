# A 20 kg flour alert gave us time to reorder

Last month, I moved our bakery from paper order slips to a shared online sheet with low-stock alerts. We had been running out of flour and throwing away unsold dough.

## Paper slips hid the warning

Before the switch, we ran out of flour twice in three weeks. In those same three weeks, we threw away 18 kg of unsold dough.

We also made two emergency trips to a wholesale store for flour. Each trip took 40 minutes. Paper slips did not give us one shared view of what was running low.

## The first alert came too late

I first set the flour alert at 10 kg. We ran out once more in the first week because the supplier needs two days to deliver, and we use about 8 kg a day.

I moved the alert to 20 kg. In the next four weeks, we had no stockouts. Waste dropped to 4 kg per week. The alert gave us a fixed point to reorder instead of waiting until flour was almost gone.

## Make the sheet part of closing

Three staff members update the free online spreadsheet. In the first week, they forgot to update it twice. I taped a printed reminder next to the till.

Checking the sheet takes five minutes at closing time. That small routine gives us a chance to see what is running low before we place an order.

Set a reorder alert that leaves enough time for your supplier to deliver, then check the sheet at closing.
