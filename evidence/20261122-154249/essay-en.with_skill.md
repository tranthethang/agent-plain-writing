# The Desk Fan

My suitcase sat in the corner, its broken zipper tied shut with twine. I had moved to a new city for university. The rented room stayed bare for three days.

My father repaired an old desk fan and packed it in a cardboard box. He said nothing about money. He explained how to change the fuse if the motor ran hot. I still wonder whether the fuse was all he meant to tell me.

On the third afternoon, I plugged in the fan. Its blades turned, stirring the air in the bare room. For the first time, the room felt lived in. I watched the blades turn.
