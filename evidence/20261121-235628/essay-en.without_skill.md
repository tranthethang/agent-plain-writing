### The Desk Fan

For the first three days, the rented room felt empty. I had moved to a new city for university, but the room still seemed like a place I was staying rather than a place that belonged to me.

My suitcase sat in the corner with its broken zipper tied shut by a piece of twine. The room was bare. My father had packed an old desk fan in a cardboard box after repairing it. He said nothing about money. He only explained how to change the fuse if the motor ran hot.

On the third afternoon, I took the fan from its box and plugged it in. It turned on.

Nothing else in the room changed. The suitcase remained in the corner, the room still bare. But with the fan running, the space felt lived in for the first time. It felt like home.
