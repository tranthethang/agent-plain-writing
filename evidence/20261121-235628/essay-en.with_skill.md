# The Fan in the Cardboard Box

My suitcase sat in the corner of the rented room, its broken zipper tied shut with twine. The room was bare. For the first three days, it stayed that way.

My father had repaired an old desk fan and packed it in a cardboard box. He said nothing about money. He told me only to change the fuse if the motor ran hot. I still wonder if that was all he meant to tell me.

On the third afternoon, I plugged in the fan. The blades began to turn, and air moved across the bare room.

For the first time, the room felt lived in. I watched the blades turn.

Added thought: “I still wonder if that was all he meant to tell me.”
