# Export a daily sales report

Export a KoraPOS daily sales summary as a CSV file.

## Before you start
- Use an iPad or Android tablet.
- Open **KoraPOS**.

## Steps
1. Tap **Reports** in the bottom bar.
2. Select **Daily Sales**.
3. Pick one date from the calendar. You can choose a date up to 90 days back.
4. Tap **Export CSV**.
5. Choose **Save to Files** or **Send to Email**.
6. Wait up to 15 seconds for `sales-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` to appear.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens after you tap **Export CSV** | Connect to Wi-Fi, then tap **Export CSV** again. |
| The file is empty (0 bytes) | No paid orders exist for that date. Pick a different date. |
| **Send to Email** fails | Ask your manager to add the store email under **Settings** > **Store Profile**. |
