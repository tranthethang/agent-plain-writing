# A shared sheet helped us catch low flour before it ran out

Last month, I switched our bakery from paper order slips to a shared online sheet with automatic low-stock alerts.

Before that, I counted flour by hand. Paper slips made it hard to see what was running low until the shelf was empty. We ran out of flour twice in three weeks, and we threw away 18 kg of unsold dough.

The change was simple: we set a reorder alert at 20 kg of flour. Now, when stock reaches that level, the alert tells us it is time to reorder. Having one shared sheet also means we can check the same stock information instead of relying on separate slips.

Since the switch, waste has dropped to 4 kg per week. I check the sheet for five minutes at closing time each day. It is a small habit, but it gives me a clearer view of what needs attention—and helps us avoid panic buying.

If you still track stock on paper, a shared sheet and a fixed reorder number can be a practical place to start.
