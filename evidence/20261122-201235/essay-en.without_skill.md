For the first three days, the rented room stayed empty, even with my things in it. My suitcase sat in the corner, its broken zipper tied shut with twine. The room was bare around it.

My father had packed an old desk fan in a cardboard box. He had repaired it before I left for university. He said nothing about money. He only explained how to change the fuse if the motor ran hot.

The box remained unopened for two days. On the third afternoon, I took the fan out and plugged it in. It turned on.

Nothing else in the room changed. The suitcase was still in the corner, and the room was still rented and bare. But with the fan running, the space felt lived in for the first time. It felt like home.
