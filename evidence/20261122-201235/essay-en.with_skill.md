# The Fan on the Third Afternoon

My suitcase sat in the corner, its broken zipper tied shut with twine. The rented room was bare. For three days, it felt empty.

My father had repaired an old desk fan and packed it in a cardboard box. He said nothing about money. He told me only to change the fuse if the motor ran hot. I still wonder whether he had more to say.

On the third afternoon, I plugged in the fan. Its blades turned, stirring air against my face. I watched them circle in the room.
