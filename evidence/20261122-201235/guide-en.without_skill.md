# Export a Daily Sales Report in KoraPOS

Use KoraPOS on an iPad or Android tablet to save or email a daily sales summary as a CSV file.

## Export the report

1. Open **KoraPOS** and tap **Reports** in the bottom bar.
2. Select **Daily Sales** and choose one date from the calendar. You can choose a date up to 90 days back.
3. Tap **Export CSV**, then choose **Save to Files** or **Send to Email**.
4. Wait up to 15 seconds for the file to appear. Its name will be `sales-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`.

## If the export doesn’t work

- **Nothing happens after you tap Export CSV:** Connect the tablet to Wi-Fi, then tap **Export CSV** again.
- **The file is empty (0 bytes):** There are no paid orders for that date. Pick a different date.
- **Send to Email fails:** Ask the manager to add the store email under **Settings** > **Store Profile**.
