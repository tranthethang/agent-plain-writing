# Export a daily sales report

Use this guide to export a daily sales summary from KoraPOS on an iPad or Android tablet.

## Before you start
- CSV is the file format used for the exported report.
- You can choose a date up to 90 days back.

## Steps
1. Open **KoraPOS**.
2. Tap **Reports** in the bottom bar.
3. Select **Daily Sales**.
4. Pick a date from the calendar.
5. Tap **Export CSV**.
6. Choose **Save to Files** or **Send to Email**.
7. Wait up to 15 seconds for `sales-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` to appear.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens after **Export CSV** | Connect the tablet to Wi-Fi, then tap **Export CSV** again. |
| The file is empty (0 bytes) | Pick a different date. No paid orders exist for that date. |
| **Send to Email** fails | Ask the manager to add the store email under **Settings** > **Store Profile**. |
