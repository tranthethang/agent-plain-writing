# English examples: poetry

## Stock words to avoid

"tapestry", "whisper", "echoes of", "ethereal", "heart aflutter", "silent tears", "tears fell", "dark night of the soul", "dance of". Allowed once, on purpose.

## Forms

- **Free verse** (default). No fixed meter or rhyme. Lines break where the reader breathes.
- **Haiku in English**: three short lines, roughly 5-7-5 syllables, one concrete image. The count is a guide, not a law. Do not force it if it bends the sentence.
- **Rhymed quatrain** (only if the user asks): rhyme ABAB or AABB. Do not rhyme if it bends the syntax or forces a filler word.

## Bad → good pairs

### 1. Naming the feeling → an object

**Bad:**
Sorrow floods my heart in the silent night,
Tears of longing fall like rain.

**Good:**
Tonight I made two cups of tea
and remembered
only one of us drinks it.

### 2. Declaring love → a detail

**Bad:** Mother, your love is deeper than the ocean and higher than the mountains.
**Good:**
The sweater you knitted
is too small now.
I still can't take it off.

### 3. A general philosophy

**Bad:** Life is a long and winding road full of challenges.
**Good:**
Worn-down heels,
and still the hill.

### 4. Closing with a moral

**Bad:**
Petals fall upon the path,
leaving only regret.
So cherish every moment.

**Good:**
Petals fall on the path.
No one bends to pick them up.
Tomorrow, the broom.

### 5. Stock adjectives

**Bad:** The ethereal moonlight whispers softly over the lonely soul.
**Good:** The moon hangs where it always hangs. One chair on the porch is empty.

### 6. A call to action

**Bad:** O young hearts, rise up and be strong!
**Good:**
The fledgling stands at the edge of the nest,
looks down,
then looks up.
