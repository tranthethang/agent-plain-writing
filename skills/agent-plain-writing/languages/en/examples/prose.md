# English examples: prose

## Clichés to avoid

"heart pounded", "heart skipped a beat", "time stood still", "a shiver ran down her spine", "let out a breath she didn't know she was holding", "eyes sparkled", "silent tears", "an overwhelming sense of", "the weight of the world".

## Bad → good pairs

### 1. Naming the emotion → action

**Bad:** She was very sad when she heard the news.
**Good:** She set down her chopsticks. The rice sat untouched. The steam was already gone.

### 2. Cliché

**Bad:** Time stood still, and his heart skipped a beat.
**Good:** He could hear the clock on the wall, a sound he hadn't noticed all year.

### 3. Abstract description → senses

**Bad:** The morning in the countryside was peaceful and beautiful.
**Good:** Morning in the village. Frost still clung to the garden. Down the lane, someone swept a doorstep, and the broom's sound trailed off.

### 4. Describing a character with adjectives

**Bad:** Grandfather was a kind, generous man who loved his grandchildren.
**Good:** Grandfather gave the biggest persimmon to the youngest child, then said he didn't like persimmons.

### 5. Dialogue that explains

**Bad:** "I'm angry because you forgot my birthday," he said sadly.
**Good:** "Eat without me tonight."
He turned his back and switched on the TV. He didn't turn up the volume.

### 6. Long sentence → rhythm

**Bad:** The old bus, which rattled slowly along the winding country road flanked on both sides by green rice fields stretching to the horizon, made the passengers feel very relaxed.
**Good:** The bus rolled slowly. The country road wound on, green fields on both sides to the horizon. Someone dozed off.

### 7. Ending on the meaning

**Bad:** And he realized that family was the most important thing in life.
**Good:** He called home. The phone rang three times before his mother picked up.

### 8. Strained metaphor

**Bad:** Sadness washed over her like a vast ocean engulfing her soul.
**Good:** She washed the same cup a third time.

## Complete sample paragraph (about 85 words)

```
Mother had been standing outside my door since afternoon. I knew because her shadow lay under the crack, perfectly still.
I didn't open it.

She knocked twice, softly, and left a plate of peeled oranges on the floor.
Her slippers faded down the hall.
I opened the door after dark. The oranges had browned at the edges. I ate every one.
```

Note: no sentence says "I was angry" or "Mother loved me". The feeling sits in the plate of oranges.
